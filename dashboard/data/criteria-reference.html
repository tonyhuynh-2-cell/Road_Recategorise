Nationally Significant State Roads

Must meet at least 2 of these criteria:

Criteria IDCriterion
S-01Comprises the Australian Government National Land Transport Network (designated priority national road freight route between capital cities)
S-02Connects from a Regional City to the nearest capital city or Metropolitan Centre
S-03Connects from a Metropolitan Centre to another Metropolitan Centre
S-04Connects from a Metropolitan Centre to the nearest International Airport
S-05Connects from a Metropolitan Centre or Major Intermodal Terminal to the closest Major Port

Mandatory criteria – must meet all:

Criteria IDCriterion
S-06Facilitates the movement of PBS Level 2B or equivalent vehicles as a minimum
—There are no load-limits placed on any assets along the route

State Roads – Remote and Regional Areas

Must meet at least 2 of these criteria:

Criteria IDCriterion
S-07Connects Metropolitan Centres, Regional Cities, Major Towns and Major Urban Centres to each other
—Connects from either a Metropolitan Centre, Regional City, Major Town or Major Urban Centre to Town Centres along a long-distance rural route
—Meets the traffic volumes and heavy vehicle percentages (see thresholds table)
S-08Connects Major Hospital, Major Ports, Major Intermodals, International Airports, Commercial, Industrial or Employment Centres to Other Centre Types
—Heavy vehicle bypass of towns

Mandatory criteria – must meet all:

Criteria IDCriterion
—There are no load limits placed on any assets along the route
—Does not parallel an existing rural State Road no closer than 20 km apart
S-09Facilitates the movement of PBS Level 1 or equivalent vehicles as a minimum

Regional Roads – Remote and Regional Areas

Must meet at least 2 of these criteria:

Criteria IDCriterion
R-01Connects Urban Centres and Town Centres to each other
—Connects at least two State Roads that significantly boost freight productivity and connectivity
—Meets the traffic volumes and heavy vehicle percentages (see thresholds table)
R-02Connects Major or Regional Hospitals, Major Ports, Major Intermodals, International and Regional Airports, Commercial, Industrial and Employment Centres to Town Centres and Urban Centres
—Emergency evacuation route or alternative resilience route
R-03Is part of the road train network (refer to NHVR maps)

Mandatory criteria – must meet all:

Criteria IDCriterion
—There are no load limits placed on any assets along the route
R-04Facilitates the movement of GML & CML 19m B-double routes (over 50 tonnes) or equivalent vehicles as a minimum

Local Roads

Local Roads primarily have an access function typically catering for first and last mile journeys. Generally, if a road does not meet the State Road or Regional Road criteria it is considered a Local Road.
